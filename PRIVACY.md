# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

Dastkhat does not collect, sell or transmit personal data to the developers. There are no analytics,
telemetry or accounts.

**Stored locally** (browser extension storage on your device only): your settings, highlights you create,
and any AI provider API keys you enter.

**AI features:** only when you click Improve, Translate, Summarize or Grammar, the text you selected is
sent directly from your browser to the AI provider endpoint **you** configured in Settings (for example
Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq, or a local Ollama server) together with your API key.
That provider's own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent otherwise, and nothing goes through servers
operated by Dastkhat.

**Page access:** the content script runs on all pages to apply Persian/RTL fonts, highlights and the
selection toolbar locally. Page content is not collected or transmitted, except the text you explicitly send
to your AI provider as described above.

**Fonts** are bundled in the extension; no remote fonts or code are loaded.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/dastyar-io/dastkhat-browser-extension/issues
